Support

R&A Legacy Program is the internal field app of R&A Legacy Construction LLC for crew time cards, clock in and out, equipment and material tickets, truck inspections, safety checklists and job cost records.

  • Forgot your PIN: ask the owner or your supervisor for a new one.
  • No signal: the app keeps working and sends everything when the connection comes back.

Contact: developer@ralegacy.co
